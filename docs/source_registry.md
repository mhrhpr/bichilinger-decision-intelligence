# Source Registry

This registry defines the evidence hierarchy for the historical reconstruction layer.

## Tier 1 — Primary / archival

### Bismarck
- Otto von Bismarck Foundation archive: https://www.bismarck-stiftung.de/
- Bismarck, *Gedanken und Erinnerungen* (Memoirs): archival/digital editions should be used for first-person evidence.

### Churchill
- International Churchill Society / Churchill Documents: https://winstonchurchill.org/
- The official multi-volume Churchill biography by Randolph Churchill and Martin Gilbert.
- Churchill's wartime papers and speeches.

### Kissinger
- Henry A. Kissinger Papers, University of Virginia: https://ead.lib.virginia.edu/vivaxtf/view?docId=uva-lib/viu03027.xml
- U.S. State Department historical diplomatic records and declassified documents.

## Tier 2 — Scholarly synthesis

### Bismarck
- Jonathan Steinberg, *Bismarck: A Life*.
- Lothar Gall, *Bismarck: The White Revolutionary*.

### Churchill
- Martin Gilbert, *Winston S. Churchill* (8-volume biography).
- Randolph Churchill / Martin Gilbert official biography project.
- Churchill, *The Second World War* — useful as a primary memoir but not treated as neutral evidence.

### Kissinger
- Henry Kissinger, *Diplomacy* — analytical memoir/history, useful but not neutral.
- Henry Kissinger, *The White House Years*.
- Henry Kissinger, *Years of Upheaval*.
- Niall Ferguson, *Kissinger, 1923–1968*.

## Tier 3 — Reference

- Encyclopaedia Britannica entries for Bismarck, Churchill and Kissinger.
- University and museum archival guides.

## Evidence policy

1. A historical fact should have at least one identifiable source.
2. High-impact decisions should ideally be triangulated across a primary/archival source and a scholarly synthesis.
3. Memoirs are evidence of what an actor later claimed, not automatic proof that the account is complete or unbiased.
4. Analytical scores are model outputs and must never be presented as historical facts.
5. Contested events retain uncertainty and competing interpretations.
6. The system must never generate invented quotations or private thoughts and attribute them to historical figures.
